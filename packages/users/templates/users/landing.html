The problem

Giving online shouldn't feel like a leap of faith

Most of us have wanted to help and then hesitated. These are the questions that stopped us.

"Is this person even real?"

Anyone can post a sad photo. Without identity checks, genuine people lose out to fakes and donors lose trust.

"Where did my money actually go?"

Funds are often handed over in one lump with no questions asked, and nobody can say what they were spent on.

"Whatever happened to that campaign?"

You give once, then silence. No updates, no outcome, no way to know you made a difference.

Our promise

Four checkpoints between your donation and the people it helps

Trust isn't a slogan here. It is a process, and every step leaves a record you can see.

  1. 01

    We verify the person

    Campaigners submit their legal name, address and ID. NGOs also submit their registration number and tax-exempt status.

  2. 02

    We verify the cause

    Evidence such as medical reports, quotes, invoices and registration papers is reviewed before a campaign can go live.

  3. 03

    We verify every expense

    Campaigners request spending against a real invoice. We check it, then pay the recipient directly, not the campaigner.

  4. 04

    We show our work

    Each decision is logged with a public note. Donors see updates, spending and outcomes on the campaign page.

Why payouts go direct: money for a hospital bill is sent to the hospital; money for school fees goes to the school. That is how we make sure your gift does exactly what it was meant to.

Honest fees

A clear target, with the platform fee included.

Campaigners enter only what they need. Vouch automatically adds a platform fee equal to 10% of that amount to the campaign target. Once raised, the campaigner can request expenses up to the amount they originally asked for; the platform fee is retained. Payment processor fees may be recorded separately.

  • Example: ₦100 needed + ₦10 fee = ₦110 target
  • Donors are not charged an extra 10% at checkout
  • Expenses are limited to the campaigner's stated need

Questions

Things people ask before they give

Can a campaigner just take the money?

No. Funds are never handed over as a lump sum. Campaigners request specific expenses with supporting documents; once reviewed, we pay the recipient directly.

What if a campaign turns out to be fraudulent?

Our review team can pause or reject a campaign at any time. Every decision is recorded in an audit log, and donations can be refunded where payouts have not yet been made.

Can I give anonymously?

Yes. Tick "give anonymously" at checkout and your name will not appear on the public donor list. We still keep your receipt for your records.

Which payment methods are supported?

Cards, bank transfer and other options provided by our licensed payment gateways. You will see what is available at checkout.

How do I know a verified badge is real?

The badge is only added after a reviewer signs off on the person and the evidence. The reviewer's public note is visible on the campaign page.
