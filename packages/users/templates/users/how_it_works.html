How it works

From a first click to a funded outcome

Here is exactly what happens, whichever side of the screen you're on.

I want to give I want to raise

For donors

Give with your eyes open

  1. 1

    Browse and filter

    Search by category, urgency or progress. Each campaign shows its verification badge, goal, deadline and who is behind it.

  2. 2

    Read the evidence

    Open the documents flagged public by the campaigner: reports, quotes, invoices. Read the reviewer's public note.

  3. 3

    Donate in under a minute

    Choose an amount and optionally give anonymously. Each campaign target already includes a 10% platform fee added to the amount needed; your donation is not increased at checkout.

  4. 4

    Get your receipt

    A receipt is stored in your account with the gateway reference so you can track or dispute any payment.

  5. 5

    Follow the story

    Campaigners post updates. Each approved expense and payout appears in the campaign's public ledger.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up as an individual campaigner or a registered NGO.

  2. 2

    Complete your profile and verify

    Add your legal name, phone number and address. NGOs add their registration number, website and tax-exempt status. Our team reviews within a few working days.

  3. 3

    Draft your campaign

    Enter the amount you need, choose a category, and write your story and end date. Vouch adds a 10% platform fee to the public target automatically: ₦100 needed becomes ₦110 to raise.

  4. 4

    Upload evidence and submit

    Attach documents that prove the need. A reviewer checks them and approves or asks for changes, with a public note either way.

  5. 5

    Go live and keep donors close

    Post updates regularly. Updates are the single biggest driver of repeat giving.

  6. 6

    Request expenses, get paid safely

    Request expenses for your stated need. The 10% fee is retained from the public target, so approved campaign expenses cannot exceed the amount you originally entered.
